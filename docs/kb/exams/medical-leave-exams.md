# Mid-Semester & Medical Leave — Exam Policies

**Document type:** Sample for CampusAssist demo

## Missing a mid-sem due to medical reasons

If a student misses a mid-semester examination because of illness:

1. Submit a **medical certificate** from a registered medical practitioner within **3 working days** of returning to campus.
2. Apply for **medical leave** through the student portal / class advisor.
3. If medical leave is approved, the Controller of Examinations may:
   - schedule a **re-test**, or
   - adjust internal assessment as per department rules.

Unapproved absence from mid-sem = **zero marks** for that mid-sem component.

## Medical leave and end-semester exams

- Medical leave does **not** automatically waive the **75% attendance** rule for end-semester eligibility.
- Long medical leave may be considered for attendance condonation (65–75% band) with Principal approval.
