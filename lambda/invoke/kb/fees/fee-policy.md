# Fee Structure & Clarifications — Demo College

**Document type:** Sample for CampusAssist demo

## Tuition (indicative, B.Tech 2026–27)

| Component | Amount (INR) |
|---|---|
| Tuition (per semester) | 75,000 |
| Development fee (annual) | 10,000 |
| Exam fee (per semester) | 2,500 |
| Application fee (one-time) | 1,000 |

Exact payable amounts appear on the fee challan generated by the accounts portal.

## Fee clarification / disputes

For fee clarification (wrong challan, scholarship mismatch, refund status):

1. Check the accounts portal first.
2. If unresolved, raise a **support ticket** with the Accounts Desk (CampusAssist can create a ticket demo ID).
3. Keep payment receipts and challan numbers ready.

## Refunds

Admission fee refunds follow the UGC / AICTE calendar published each year. Processing may take 4–6 weeks after approval.
