# Examination Rules — Demo College

**Document type:** Sample for CampusAssist demo

## Semester examinations

- End-semester (final) exams are held at the end of each semester.
- Mid-semester (mid-sem) exams are held roughly halfway through the semester.
- Students must carry a valid ID card and hall ticket into the exam hall.

## Attendance requirement for semester exams

- **Minimum 75% attendance** in each subject is required to be eligible for the end-semester examination.
- Students between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation with supporting reasons; approval is not automatic.
- Below 65% attendance: student is **detained** and not permitted to write the end-semester exam for that subject unless the Academic Council grants a special exception.

## Mid-semester exams

- Mid-sem marks contribute to internal assessment.
- Missing a mid-sem without approved leave results in zero for that component unless a re-test is sanctioned.

## Malpractice

Any malpractice leads to cancellation of the paper and disciplinary action under the code of conduct.
