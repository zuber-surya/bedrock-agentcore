# Campus Placement — Demo College

**Institution:** Demo Institute of Technology (DIT)  
**Office:** Training & Placement (T&P) Cell  
**Document type:** Sample for CampusAssist Placement agent demo (not an official college policy)

## Placement process

1. Register on the T&P portal at the start of the final / pre-final year.
2. Complete mandatory pre-placement training (aptitude + soft skills).
3. Apply to open campus drives before each company’s deadline.
4. Attend shortlisted rounds (test / GD / interview) as scheduled.
5. Accept offers only through the T&P portal; parallel offers follow institute policy.

## Eligibility

- Minimum **CGPA 6.5** (or 65%) with **no active backlogs** at the time of the drive (some companies may require higher).
- Students with more than **2 standing arrears** are not eligible for core / product drives until cleared.
- Minimum **75% attendance** in the current semester is required to sit for campus interviews.
- Internship-to-PPO conversions must be reported to T&P within **7 days** of the offer letter.

## Drive season (indicative 2026–27)

| Window | Focus |

| October–December | Core engineering + analytics |
