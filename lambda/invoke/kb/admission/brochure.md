# B.Tech Admission Brochure — Demo College

**Institution:** Demo Institute of Technology (DIT)  
**Academic year:** 2026–27  
**Document type:** Sample for CampusAssist demo (not an official college policy)

## Programs

- B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
- B.Tech Electronics & Communication (ECE)
- B.Tech Mechanical Engineering (ME)

## Application window

- Applications open: **1 March 2026**
- **Last date for B.Tech admission applications: 30 June 2026**
- Late applications are not accepted after the closing date unless the Principal grants a written exception.

## Documents required for admission

Candidates must upload / submit:

1. Class 10 mark sheet
2. Class 12 mark sheet (or equivalent)
3. Transfer certificate / school leaving certificate
4. Passport-size photographs (2)
5. Government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar / passport)
6. Caste / category certificate (if applicable)
7. Entrance exam score card (if applicable)

Incomplete document sets delay seat confirmation.

## Eligibility (summary)

- Minimum 45% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics in Class 12 (40% for reserved categories as per institute norms).
- Age and domicile rules follow state / AICTE guidelines published each year.
