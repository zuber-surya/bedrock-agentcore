# Admission Process — Demo College

**Document type:** Sample for CampusAssist demo

## Steps

1. Register on the online admission portal.
2. Fill the application and pay the application fee.
3. Upload required documents listed in the admission brochure.
4. Appear for counselling / seat allotment as per schedule.
5. Pay the first-semester fee to confirm the seat.
6. Report for orientation with originals for verification.

## Important dates (2026)

| Milestone | Date |
|---|---|
| Portal opens | 1 March 2026 |
| Application closes | **30 June 2026** |
| First counselling | Mid-July 2026 |
| Classes commence | August 2026 |

## Seat confirmation

A seat is confirmed only after fee payment and document verification. Provisional allotment without fee payment expires in 7 working days.
