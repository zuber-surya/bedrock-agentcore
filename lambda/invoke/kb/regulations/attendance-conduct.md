# Academic Regulations — Attendance & Conduct

**Document type:** Sample for CampusAssist demo

## Attendance

- Students must maintain **at least 75% attendance** in every credited course.
- Attendance is calculated from the first instructional day to the last instructional day of the semester.
- Biometric / roll-call records maintained by the department are authoritative.

## Leave of absence

- Casual leave: apply through class advisor (limited days per semester).
- **Medical leave:** requires medical certificate; apply within 3 working days of return.
- Medical leave days are recorded but do not automatically count as “present” for the 75% calculation unless condonation is granted.

## Code of conduct (summary)

- Respect faculty, staff, and peers.
- No ragging; zero tolerance.
- Campus ID must be worn during academic hours.
- Damage to property may lead to fines and disciplinary action.

## Grievances

Academic grievances go first to the class advisor, then Head of Department, then Dean (Academics).
