# Academic Regulations — General

**Document type:** Sample for CampusAssist demo

## Credit system

DIT follows a semester credit system. Students must earn the minimum credits prescribed in the scheme of study to graduate.

## Promotion

Promotion to the next year requires clearing backlog limits published by the Controller of Examinations each year.

## Revaluation

Students may apply for revaluation of end-semester answer scripts within the window announced after results. Mid-sem scripts are generally not revalued.

## Withdrawal

Course withdrawal must be completed before the mid-sem week; later withdrawals need Dean approval.
